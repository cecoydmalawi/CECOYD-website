Who we are

Local action. Youth leadership. Lasting change.

CECOYD is a youth-led, non-profit and non-political organization established in 2014 and working to improve the socio-economic status of rural youth through local resources, partnerships and practical opportunities.

◎

Our Vision

A Malawi where young people have the opportunities, knowledge and agency to build independent and responsible lives.

↗

Our Mission

To empower young people and communities through education, enterprise, civic engagement and partnerships that create practical pathways to progress.

✦

Our Approach

We listen locally, build partnerships, strengthen youth capacity and turn community ideas into measurable action.

What we do

Programmes designed around real youth needs.
