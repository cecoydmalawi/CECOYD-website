Centre for Concerned Youth in Development (CECOYD) is a Malawian youth-focused, non-governmental, non-profit and non-political local organization working to improve the socio-economic status of rural youth.

Who we are

Rooted in communities. Led by youth.

CECOYD was established in 2014 and registered with the Malawi Government under the Trustees Incorporation Act 2000 in 2016. The organization aims to improve the socio-economic status of rural youth so they can be responsible, accountable and economically independent through sustainable use of locally available resources.

◎

Mission

To ensure vibrant youth who can participate in all aspects of socio-economic development through collaboration with other development stakeholders.

◇

Vision

Youth who are socially and economically empowered and can effectively contribute positively to the socio-economic activities of their families, communities and nation.

✦

Core values

Heart & passion; Voice, Head & Action; Human Rights; Empowerment; Responsibility; Dialogue & Cooperation; Partnership; and Legitimacy.

Our focus

Seven critical areas of youth development.

01

Education

Formal and informal education, motivational talks, leadership workshops and support for school-going young people.

02

Health

Preventive health, sexual and reproductive health and rights, youth-friendly health engagement and community awareness.

03

Agriculture & Entrepreneurship

Youth farming clubs, agri-business entrepreneurship, skills development, cooperatives and access to enterprise opportunities.

04

Environment

Environmental education, conservation, tree planting, regeneration and community clean-up initiatives.

05

Democracy

Youth participation, civic education, political debates, voting awareness and public accountability.

06

Human Rights

Rights awareness and strengthening the voice and participation of young people and minority groups.

07

Participation

Dialogue, interface meetings, panel discussions and platforms where youth concerns can be raised with stakeholders.

R

Research & Learning

The Youth Development and Agricultural Research Centre (YDAR-Centre) supports youth-centred research and student research or practicum.

Programmes & platforms

Turning youth potential into practical opportunity.

CECOYD combines education, economic empowerment, civic participation, research, health and environmental action with collaboration across communities and development stakeholders.

Education

YESE

Youth Economic Empowerment through Supportive Education has facilitated more than 200 children and young people back to primary and secondary school since 2017. The programme has also supported deserving youth pursuing fields including engineering, commerce, health, education, agriculture, social work and youth development.

Agriculture & enterprise

Youth Soybean & Agribusiness

Since 2021, CECOYD has worked with SunHawk Energy International and LUANAR on youth soybean production and value addition, alongside youth farming clubs, agribusiness entrepreneurship and cooperative development.

YDAR-Centre
